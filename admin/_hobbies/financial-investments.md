I started investing when I began working at [Siemens](/experience/siemens/) — more precisely, once I had closed my **emergency fund**. Since then I invest part of my income in:

- **ETFs**, through Degiro;
- **Cryptocurrencies** — BTC and ETH — through Coinbase;
- **Other instruments**, such as **Certificados de Aforro** (Portuguese state savings certificates), part of which make up my emergency fund.
