In **August 2019** I attended the **3rd edition of the Security Summer School** (SecThon), a cybersecurity programme organised by **Multicert**, from **21 to 23 August 2019** — 20 hours in total. The certificate was issued in Oeiras on 23 August 2019.

## Topics

- **Cyber intelligence**
- **Malware analysis** (theory and practice)
- **Face to face with IoT security** (theory and practice)
- **Social engineering**
- **Hands-on pentesting** (theory and practice)
- **Cyber economics**
- **Infrastructure security** (theory and practice)
- **Cybercrime trends**
