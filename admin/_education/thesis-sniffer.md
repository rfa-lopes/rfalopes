**SNIFFER: Dynamic Test Case Generator for Information Flow Control using SNITCH** is my Master's thesis, graded **17/20**.

It addresses the testing of information-flow properties — whether sensitive information can leak out of a program — through a dynamic test-case generator that also quantifies leaks.
